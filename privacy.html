Privacy Policy — Aten Clips

Last updated: November 2026

Aten Clips respects user privacy and operates exclusively for the personal use of its sole developer and account owner.

  • No Third-Party Data Collection: This application does not collect, store, sell, or share personal data from any external users.
  • Authentication Tokens: OAuth access tokens generated via TikTok Login Kit are stored locally on the owner's private server solely to upload draft videos to the owner's TikTok inbox.
  • Data Deletion: Temporary video files are automatically deleted from the local server immediately after upload completion.
